        How to read this

        Break-even is the win rate needed at the offered price. The market baseline removes the bookmaker’s margin by normalizing the two implied probabilities. Gap is your selected estimate minus that baseline, in percentage points.

        Expected return is probability × decimal odds − 1 per unit staked. It is conditional on the probability estimate, before commissions and other fees. It is not a verified betting edge. The audited candidate has separate calibration and later-fight evaluation, but has not demonstrated a betting edge.
