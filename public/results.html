What remains unproven

Historical accuracy does not establish an advantage over market prices. There is no demonstrated betting edge, and current injuries, camps and late changes are not fully captured by fight records.

  • Future performance needs predictions recorded before the outcomes are known.
  • Historical source snapshots may contain information revised after a fight.
  • Small groups of high-confidence predictions cannot establish reliable high-confidence performance.
